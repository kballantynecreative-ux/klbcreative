Your website: setup guide

You do Part 1 once (about 30–45 minutes). After that you only use Part 2, which is a simple form.

Part 1: one-time setup

Step 1. Make a free GitHub account

Go to github.com and sign up. GitHub is just a safe folder on the internet where your website files live. It is free. Remember your username.

Step 2. Make a place for your website

  1. Click the + at the top right, then New repository.
  2. Name it klbcreative. Choose Public (your website is public anyway). Click Create repository.

Step 3. Upload your website files

  1. Unzip the folder I gave you on your computer.
  2. On your new repository page, click uploading an existing file.
  3. Open the unzipped folder and drag everything inside it (the admin, content and images folders, plus index.html and CNAME) into the browser window. Wait for the upload to finish.
  4. Click Commit changes at the bottom.

Step 4. Tell the admin form which account is yours

  1. In your repository, click the admin folder, then config.yml.
  2. Click the pencil icon (edit).
  3. Find YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME/klbcreative and replace YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME with your real GitHub username. Click Commit changes.

Step 5. Turn the website on

  1. In your repository click Settings, then Pages (left side).
  2. Under "Build and deployment", choose Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder / (root), then Save.
  3. After a minute or two, a link like yourusername.github.io/klbcreative appears. Your site is live there.

Step 6. Use your own domain, klbcreative.net

  1. On the same Pages screen, type klbcreative.net under Custom domain and click Save.
  2. Log in to the company where you bought your domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Shopify, etc.) and open its DNS settings.
  3. Delete any old records that point to Shopify. Add four A records for @ pointing to: 185.199.108.153, 185.199.109.153, 185.199.110.153, 185.199.111.153. Add one CNAME record for www pointing to yourusername.github.io.
  4. Wait (from a few minutes up to a day). Back on the GitHub Pages screen, tick Enforce HTTPS when it becomes available.

Check these numbers against GitHub's own help page (search "GitHub Pages custom domain apex") in case they have changed. If you get stuck on this step, tell me which company holds your domain and I will walk you through it.

Step 7. Make your key for the admin form

  1. On GitHub click your picture (top right), then Settings, then at the bottom left Developer settings, then Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens, then Generate new token.
  2. Name it website. Choose the longest expiration offered.
  3. Under Repository access choose Only select repositories and pick klbcreative.
  4. Under Permissions, open Repository permissions, find Contents and set it to Read and write.
  5. Click Generate token and copy it. Save it somewhere safe (a password manager or a private note). GitHub only shows it once. Treat it like a password.

When the token expires, make a new one the same way.

Part 2: adding or changing a painting (every time after)

  1. Go to klbcreative.net/admin.
  2. The first time, choose to sign in with GitHub using a token and paste your key.
  3. Click Paintings, then All paintings.
  4. To add: click Add painting. Type the title, size and price, upload photos, and switch on Sold when it sells.
  5. To change one (new price, mark sold, swap a photo): click it, edit, done.
  6. Click Save or Publish. Your site updates in about 1–2 minutes (refresh the page to see it).

Photo tip: phone photos are very large and slow the site down. Before uploading, shrink them to about 1600 pixels wide (the Photos app, or the free site squoosh.app, can do this).

The first photo in the list is the one shown on the page. Visitors click it to see all the others.

Other things you can do

  • Change the colors: in GitHub open index.html, click the pencil, and near the top you will see the site's colors: --bg (cream), --paper (white), --ink (black) and --accent (green, currently #9CA812, matched to your logo; --accent-text is a darker version of it used for small green text). Change the code after any of them, then Commit changes.
  • Make the contact form send you email: sign up free at formspree.io, make a form, and replace YOUR_FORM_ID in index.html with your form's ID. Until then, "send" opens the visitor's email app.
  • Undo a mistake: GitHub keeps every earlier version. Ask me and I will show you how to go back.
